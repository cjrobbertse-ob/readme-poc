Once you're logged in to ReadMe, you'll arrive at your dashboard home where you'll see a list of all of the projects that you're either a <Glossary>Project Owner</Glossary> or <Glossary>Project Admin</Glossary> of. ## How to Bake a Perfect Cake 🍰

Baking a delicious cake is a rewarding experience that brings joy to both the baker and those who get to enjoy the final result. Here's a comprehensive guide to creating your own homemade masterpiece:

### Essential Ingredients

- **2 cups all-purpose flour** - The foundation of your cake
- **1¾ cups granulated sugar** - For sweetness and structure
- **¾ cup unsalted butter** (room temperature) - Adds richness and moisture
- **3 large eggs** - Provides structure and binding
- **1 cup whole milk** - Creates a tender crumb
- **2½ teaspoons baking powder** - The leavening agent for lift
- **1 teaspoon vanilla extract** - Essential flavor enhancement
- **½ teaspoon salt** - Balances and intensifies flavors

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prepare Your Workspace**
   - Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C)
   - Grease two 9-inch round cake pans
   - Line the bottom with parchment paper for easy removal

2. **Mix the Dry Ingredients**
   - In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt
   - Set aside for later use

3. **Cream Butter and Sugar**
   - In a large bowl, beat butter and sugar until light and fluffy (about 3-4 minutes)
   - This creates air pockets for a tender cake

4. **Add Eggs and Vanilla**
   - Beat in eggs one at a time, ensuring each is fully incorporated
   - Mix in vanilla extract until combined

5. **Alternate Wet and Dry Ingredients**
   - Add 1/3 of the flour mixture, mixing until just combined
   - Pour in 1/2 of the milk, mix gently
   - Add another 1/3 of flour, then remaining milk, then final flour
   - Stop mixing as soon as ingredients are combined to avoid tough cake

6. **Bake to Perfection**
   - Divide batter evenly between prepared pans
   - Bake for 25-30 minutes until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
   - Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks

### Pro Tips for Success

- **Room temperature ingredients** blend more easily and create better texture
- **Don't overmix** the batter - this develops gluten and makes cake tough  
- **Test for doneness** with a toothpick or cake tester
- **Cool completely** before frosting to prevent melting

Your homemade cake will be the perfect centerpiece for any celebration!
