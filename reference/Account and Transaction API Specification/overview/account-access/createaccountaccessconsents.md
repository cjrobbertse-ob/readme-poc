## Chocolate Bars

Chocolate bars are one of the world's most beloved confections, combining the rich, complex flavors of cacao with various ingredients to create diverse taste experiences. These sweet treats have evolved from simple cocoa-based confections to sophisticated culinary creations.

### Types of Chocolate Bars

**Dark Chocolate**
- Contains higher percentages of cocoa solids (typically 50-90%)
- Offers intense, sometimes bitter flavors with earthy undertones
- Rich in antioxidants and generally considered the healthiest option

**Milk Chocolate**
- Blends cocoa with milk powder or condensed milk
- Provides a creamy, sweet taste that appeals to many palates
- Most popular variety worldwide

**White Chocolate**
- Made from cocoa butter, sugar, and milk solids (no cocoa solids)
- Delivers a sweet, vanilla-like flavor profile
- Technically not "chocolate" by some definitions

### Popular Ingredients and Variations

Modern chocolate bars often incorporate additional ingredients like:
- Nuts (almonds, hazelnuts, peanuts)
- Dried fruits (raisins, cranberries, orange peel)
- Caramel, nougat, or other fillings
- Sea salt, spices, or herbs
- Cookies, wafers, or crispy elements

### Quality Factors

The best chocolate bars are characterized by:
- High-quality cacao beans from specific regions
- Proper tempering for smooth texture and snap
- Balanced sweetness that doesn't overpower the chocolate flavor
- Fresh ingredients and proper storage
